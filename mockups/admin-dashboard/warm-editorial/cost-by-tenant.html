Reading the Roll.

For the new desk operator

The tenant rollup runs nightly at 02:00 UTC. It joins the per-service cost cache against the audio_session table to apportion shared infrastructure cost back to the tenant whose sessions consumed it. Cost-per-session for the streaming tier is materially higher than for the batch tier, so a single tenant heavy on live transcription will show outsized share even at modest session counts.

Acme Audio Group is the design partner; their concentration is expected and contractually accounted for. Brookline Therapy Collective is the second tenant on the Pro tier whose growth the team is tracking against the $30/seat Team-tier upsell threshold; expect them to cross in June.
