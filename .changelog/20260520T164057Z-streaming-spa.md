- `services/admin`: `/realtime` now uses true-streaming over WebSocket + AudioWorklet per design v7 (`docs/design/realtime-streaming-transcription.md`). The chunked-batch `realtime-session.ts` is replaced by `streaming-session.ts` (JSON+base64 frame envelope per CRIT-02, 10 Hz catch-up burst flush per DEG-03, 60 s spawn-state ping per HIGH-05, ping-echo per CRIT-05, final-chunk re-assembly, parent_session_id + prompt_seed_text 110-min reconnect plumbing). Browser must support `AudioContext({sampleRate: 16000})` (Chromium + Firefox today; Safari unsupported in v1).
